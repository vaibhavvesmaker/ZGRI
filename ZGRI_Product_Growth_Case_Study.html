03 · PRODUCT WORKFLOW

Design one continuous journey—from operator setup through portfolio proof.

The product is a multi-user operating system: operator data and requirements, resident choices, carrier evidence, rules, exception handling, and auditable outcome reporting must stay synchronized throughout the lease.

Configure

Map operator, property, lease, unit, resident, coverage, notice, and waiver requirements.

Activate

Trigger residents at the correct lease event through the approved channel, language, and deadline.

Choose

Purchase HO4, connect an existing policy, or enter an approved operator protection path.

Verify

Match policyholder, address, effective dates, coverage limits, and required interested-party details.

Monitor & resolve

Detect change or lapse, apply deterministic rules, automate clear cases, and route ambiguity with evidence.

Prove & expand

Report protection, resident experience, operations, and economics by property; then roll out the next cohort.

04 · MEASUREMENT SYSTEM

Measure the Zero-Gap promise—not just policies sold.

A policy sale captures one transaction. The product promise is continuous protection across the eligible lease period. My proposed North Star therefore measures protection at the unit-day level and pairs it with resident, reliability, operational, growth, and economic guardrails.
Proposed North Star

Continuous Protection Rate

The percentage of eligible occupied unit-days that end with either a verified compliant policy or an approved protection path in effect. The raw count beneath it is Protected Unit-Days; the rate makes portfolios and cohorts comparable.

Gate 1 · Instrument

Can we trust the baseline?

Metric contract, event lineage, launch-stage timestamps, exception taxonomy, and quality audit are complete.

Gate 2 · Prove

Did one intervention move the outcome?

A controlled cohort shows improvement with no unacceptable change in false decisions, complaints, incidents, or loss indicators.

Gate 3 · Repeat

Can the next property launch more easily?

The solution is reusable across a second operator/property cohort without equivalent custom engineering or operational work.

06 · EXECUTION MODEL

Use the first 90 days to learn, prove, and operationalize.

I would arrive with a disciplined decision process—not a prewritten feature roadmap. The initial goal is to establish truth, identify the binding constraint, run one evidence-generating intervention, and convert the learning into a repeatable launch and expansion system.
Days 1–30 · Learn

Establish the product truth

Shadow operators, property teams, implementation, support, risk/compliance, data, and engineering. Define the journey, event model, baseline funnel, and top exception Pareto.

Days 31–60 · Prove

Attack one binding constraint

Select the highest-impact launch, activation, or exception problem. Define hypothesis, cohort, primary metric, guardrails, owner, and rollback; run the pilot.

Days 61–90 · Repeat

Turn evidence into a system

Codify the implementation template, telemetry, operating runbook, operator scorecard, decision log, and scale/no-scale recommendation.
